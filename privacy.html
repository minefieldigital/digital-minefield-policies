Privacy Policy

Effective 4 September 2026 · Digital Minefield

§1

What this application is

This is an internal tool built and operated solely by Digital Minefield to manage Digital Minefield's own official social media accounts (including TikTok). It is not distributed publicly and is not intended for use by anyone outside Digital Minefield.

§2

Data we collect

This application does not collect, store, or share personal data belonging to any user other than the single account(s) it was built to operate. It does not access, request, or process data from third parties.

§3

How access tokens are used

Access and refresh tokens issued by platform APIs are used exclusively to publish content on behalf of Digital Minefield's own account. They are never used to read, message, or act on behalf of any other account.

§4

Storage & security

Credentials are stored in a local configuration file on Digital Minefield's own equipment, accessible only to Digital Minefield. They are never logged, transmitted to a third party, or stored in any shared or public system.
